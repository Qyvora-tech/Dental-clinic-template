# Inner-page design direction

## Reference

- Source: https://www.dentalaesthetics.pk/
- Captured: 2026-10-29 via Firecrawl (homepage markdown, branding data, and full-page screenshot).
- Use: visual inspiration only. No reference copy, imagery, or branded assets are reused.

## Observed patterns

- A restrained, editorial rhythm: short headline, compact supporting copy, then a large image or visual proof point.
- Clear conversion points are repeated without making every section feel like a sales card.
- Service navigation is grouped by intent, with compact labels and generous whitespace.
- Photography carries the emotional weight; text stays scannable and avoids long blocks.
- Strong contrast between a dark opening canvas and light content sections creates a premium clinic feel.
- Detail pages follow a readable sequence: breadcrumb, focused H1, one short explanatory block, a large treatment image, then concise treatment chapters.

## City Smiles adaptation

- Preserve the existing City Smiles navy, ice, mint, teal, Fraunces/Karla typography, and local image assets.
- Apply the text-first / image-second sequence to inner service and consultation pages only.
- Treat each featured treatment as a small editorial chapter: category label, clear title, short clinical context, action, then the supporting image.
- Keep copy to short, clinically responsible summaries; do not invent clinicians, reviews, outcomes, or credentials.
- Keep the homepage source and shared homepage sections unchanged.
